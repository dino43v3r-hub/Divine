Title: Unresolved Suffering Test
Type: Counterexample pressure

This test asks whether the divine pattern still matters when suffering does not resolve quickly.
A person prays, waits, receives silence, and still lives with chronic pain, grief, trauma, and unanswered questions.
The pattern must not pretend that every lament becomes visible healing right away.
Practical theology must make room for honest lament, patient presence, care, and hope without denial.
