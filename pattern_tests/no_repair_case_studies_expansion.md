# No-Repair Case Studies Expansion

Type: hard unresolved-suffering pressure tests
Source status: practical lived-source framework, counterargument

These cases are intentionally hard. They should weaken or revise the pattern if
the project cannot speak truthfully without forcing hope, blaming victims, or
calling symbolic witness repair.

## Wrongful Conviction With No Exoneration In Life

- Case pressure: a person dies in prison while evidence of innocence remains
  ignored.
- Pattern stress: truth, justice, and repair do not arrive within the person's
  life.
- Rival readings: legal failure, racism, poverty, prosecutorial incentives,
  public indifference, and institutional self-protection.
- Required revision: do not claim redemption if the harmed person never receives
  justice. At most, name witness, protest, and unresolved hope.

## Abuse Survivor Whose Community Protects The Abuser

- Case pressure: a survivor reports harm and the religious community preserves
  reputation over truth.
- Pattern stress: church language becomes part of the harm.
- Rival readings: power, fear, institutional loyalty, misogyny, clericalism,
  family systems, and coercive forgiveness.
- Required revision: weaken any pattern that treats community as communion
  without accountability.

## Refugee Family Permanently Separated

- Case pressure: war, policy, and bureaucracy separate family members with no
  reunion.
- Pattern stress: hospitality, belonging, and repair remain mostly absent.
- Rival readings: geopolitics, nationalism, immigration law, trauma, scarcity,
  and racialized borders.
- Required revision: do not convert endurance into resolution. The strongest
  faithful response may be material aid, advocacy, and memory.

## Chronic Pain Without Diagnosis Or Relief

- Case pressure: a patient lives with disabling pain, disbelief, financial loss,
  and no clear treatment.
- Pattern stress: healing and meaning language can become cruel.
- Rival readings: medical uncertainty, gender bias, poverty, insurance systems,
  social isolation, and clinical limits.
- Required revision: use lament, presence, and practical care. Do not claim
  hidden purpose.

## Community After Environmental Loss

- Case pressure: land, graves, language, and livelihood are lost to extraction
  or climate damage.
- Pattern stress: creation-care language arrives too late for restoration.
- Rival readings: economics, colonial land theft, regulatory failure, corporate
  power, and global inequality.
- Required revision: distinguish repair from memorial. If land is not restored
  and people remain displaced, keep the pattern weakened.
