# Dementia Identity Lament Test

Type: hard unresolved-suffering case study
Source status: practical lived source, pastoral counterargument

A person with dementia loses memory, speech, independence, and shared identity
with family. Prayer, medicine, care, and community may preserve dignity, but the
condition may continue to decline.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Meaning cannot depend only on cognitive clarity or productivity.
- The image of God must not be reduced to intelligence, memory, or usefulness.
- Community practice means patient presence, bodily care, protection from
  neglect, and support for caregivers.
- Transformation may belong more to the surrounding community's fidelity than to
  visible improvement in the person who is ill.

Failure condition:

The pattern does not hold if it requires visible recovery, treats decline as
spiritual failure, or cannot name dignity when memory and speech fade.
