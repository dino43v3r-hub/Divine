# Child Loss And Lament Test

Type: Hard unresolved-suffering case study
Source status: practical lived source

A family loses a child. Prayer does not reverse the death. Friends offer
explanations that wound more than they help. The family faces grief, anger,
silence, memory, anniversaries, and the fear that hope has become impossible.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Any easy order-to-meaning sequence is morally dangerous here.
- Lament must be allowed to remain lament.
- Community practice means presence, meals, silence, memorial, counseling, and
  protection from cruel explanations.
- Hope, if present, must be humble and patient, not a demand that grief end on a
  schedule.

Failure condition:

The pattern fails if it explains the death as useful, necessary, deserved, or
spiritually simple.
