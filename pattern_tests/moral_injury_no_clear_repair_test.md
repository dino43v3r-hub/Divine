# Moral Injury No Clear Repair Test

Type: hard unresolved-suffering and moral injury case study
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A person participates in, witnesses, or fails to prevent serious harm in war,
medicine, ministry, policing, family systems, or institutional life. Years later
there is confession, therapy, prayer, and changed behavior, but no way to undo
the harm and no guarantee that the harmed people experience repair.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Transformation cannot be measured only by the offender's relief.
- Moral response requires truth, restitution where possible, public
  accountability, and care for those harmed.
- Forgiveness may never be offered, and reconciliation may be impossible.
- Community practice must resist protecting reputation over victims.

Failure condition:

The pattern fails if it treats private peace as full redemption while victims
still carry unrepaired harm.
