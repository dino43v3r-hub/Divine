# Chronic Illness Without Resolution Test

Type: Hard unresolved-suffering case study
Source status: practical lived source

A person lives for years with chronic pain, medical uncertainty, fatigue,
financial strain, and unanswered prayer. The condition does not become a neat
testimony. Friends drift away. Work becomes unstable. Worship is difficult
because the body itself feels like a site of loss.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Creation and order are hard to see when the body feels unreliable.
- Meaning cannot be forced by saying "everything happens for a reason."
- Moral response begins with truthful presence, practical care, accessible
  community, and refusal to blame the sufferer.
- Transformation may look like endurance, adjusted hope, advocacy, and small
  practices of care rather than visible cure.

Failure condition:

The pattern fails if it treats healing as guaranteed, uses suffering as proof of
spiritual weakness, or turns unresolved pain into a tidy illustration.
