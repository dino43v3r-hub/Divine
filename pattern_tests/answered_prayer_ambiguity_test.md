# Answered Prayer Ambiguity Test

Type: Rival explanation and disconfirming pressure
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A person prays for work, healing, reconciliation, or safety and later receives
what looks like an answer. The event may also have a natural explanation:
ordinary probability, medical treatment, social support, timing, confirmation
bias, selective memory, or coincidence.

Pressure on the pattern:

- The pattern cannot treat every meaningful event as proof of God.
- Father, Son, and Holy Spirit language must not erase ordinary causes.
- Gratitude may be faithful, but evidence and humility still matter.
- A psychological explanation or social explanation may account for some of the
  perceived pattern without disconfirming all faith.

Failure condition:

The pattern fails if it calls coincidence proof, refuses rival explanation,
ignores evidence, or uses answered prayer stories to pressure people whose
prayers remain unanswered.
