Title: Non-Christian Comparison Test
Type: Comparative pressure

Judaism, Islam, Hindu traditions, Buddhist traditions, secular humanism, atheism, Stoic philosophy, and Indigenous traditions may also describe order, meaning, ethics, community, and transformation.
This does not disprove the Christian pattern.
It tests what is uniquely Trinitarian: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit as distinct persons and one God.
The pattern must avoid flattening other traditions into Christian categories.

Additional pressure:

Some testimonies from many religious and nonreligious backgrounds report dreams,
visions, unexpected attraction to Jesus, scripture encounters, or conversion
movements toward Christian faith. These accounts should be studied, but they
must pass testimony, comparative-religion, psychology, anthropology, and
Trinitarian discernment filters. They may support a research question about
Christ's witness across religious boundaries. They do not by themselves prove
that Jesus or the Holy Spirit is present in every other religious tradition in
the same way.
