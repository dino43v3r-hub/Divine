# Secular Transformation Comparison Test

Type: Non-Christian comparison, rival explanation, and practical case study
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A person leaves addiction, shame, isolation, and destructive habits through a
secular recovery group, counseling, medication, friendship, disciplined
practice, and honest repair. There is order, meaning, community, moral
response, and transformation without explicit Christian confession.

Pressure on the pattern:

- The pattern must not claim every recovery process as secretly Christian.
- Secular humanist and psychological explanation may describe real repair.
- Christian interpretation must identify what is specifically Trinitarian
  without flattening the person's own account.
- Practical theology should learn from the good fruit without exaggerating its
  uniqueness.

Failure condition:

The pattern would fail if it needs to rename every non-Christian good as
Christian evidence, or if it cannot distinguish shared human wisdom from
Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
