# Poverty Generational Pressure Test

Type: hard injustice and unresolved-suffering case study
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A family works across generations but remains trapped by low wages, unstable
housing, medical debt, underfunded schools, unsafe neighborhoods, and weak legal
protection. Personal virtue helps but does not remove structural barriers.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Moral response must include systems, policy, labor, debt, housing, education,
  and public justice.
- Community care is real but may be too small for structural harm.
- Transformation cannot be reduced to private attitude or inspirational stories.
- Rival explanations include economics, history, policy, race, geography, and
  inherited disadvantage.

Failure condition:

The pattern fails if it praises endurance while ignoring exploitation, or if it
uses spiritual language to excuse preventable poverty.
