# Top Five Pattern Competition Matrix

Type: pressure test
Source status: active competition rule

The top-five divine-pattern families should compete before they are synthesized.
The project should ask which pattern explains the evidence most faithfully,
which pattern is most useful in practical theology, and which pattern weakens
under pressure.

## Shared Pressure Tests

- unresolved suffering where no repair is visible,
- spiritual abuse or institutional misuse,
- other religious traditions and testimonies,
- gifts of the Holy Spirit across cultures with abuse safeguards,
- biblical-language and original-context constraints,
- history, power, empire, and memory,
- psychology of pattern perception and confirmation bias,
- mathematics, statistics, logic, and science guardrails,
- daily-life usefulness under stress.

## Competition Rule

A pattern gains disciplined strength when it:

- is supported by reviewed evidence,
- preserves theological distinction,
- names counter-readings,
- survives hard cases without becoming vague,
- leads to practical love, justice, humility, worship, and truthful hope.

A pattern weakens when it:

- ignores suffering,
- borrows from other traditions without respect,
- uses science or math as proof beyond scope,
- makes the Holy Spirit a vague force,
- becomes only psychology or only symbolism,
- cannot guide real daily action.
