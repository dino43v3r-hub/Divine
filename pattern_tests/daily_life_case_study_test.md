Title: Daily Life Case Study Test
Type: Practical theology pressure

Case study: a person is burned out at work, angry about family conflict, anxious about money, and disconnected from church community.
The pattern must become practical: notice the real tension, name the longing, discern one moral response, ask for help, repair one relationship, rest, pray, and serve one neighbor.
If the pattern cannot guide a daily life decision, it is only an idea.
