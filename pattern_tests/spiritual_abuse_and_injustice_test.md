# Spiritual Abuse And Injustice Test

Type: Hard unresolved-suffering and corruption case study
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A church or religious leader uses spiritual language to control people, hide
abuse, protect reputation, silence victims, and call accountability divisive.
The victims experience trauma, loss of trust, isolation, and confusion about
God, scripture, prayer, and community.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Worship and community language can be corrupted.
- Trinitarian language cannot be used to protect domination.
- Moral response requires truth-telling, investigation, accountability,
  protection of victims, restitution where possible, and refusal to rush
  forgiveness.
- Transformation must include structural repair, not only private emotion.

Failure condition:

The pattern fails if it protects institutions over wounded people, treats
forgiveness as silence, or uses theology to avoid justice.
