cannot face these tests must be weakened, revised, or rejected.

| Pressure area | Pattern pressure | Failure condition |

## Rival Explanation Checklist

- Psychology: pattern perception, attachment, trauma coping, cognition, desire,
  group identity, and meaning-making may explain repeated motifs.
- Sociology: ritual, institution, status, power, social repair, and group
  cohesion may explain durable practices.
- History: memory, empire, reform, violence, migration, and trauma may explain
  recurring themes without proving divine disclosure.
- Textual criticism: variant readings, genre, redaction, translation, and
  reception history may weaken overconfident text claims.
- Comparative religion: similar practices can arise from shared human needs
  while preserving real doctrinal difference.
- Ordinary pattern perception: people may find pattern because they are looking
  for it; base rates and comparison cases remain necessary.
