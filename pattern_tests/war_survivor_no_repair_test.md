# War Survivor No Repair Test

Type: hard unresolved-suffering case study
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A survivor of war loses family, home, safety, language community, and trust in
institutions. Years later there is no full restitution, no public confession
from those responsible, and no stable return to the life that was destroyed.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Order has been shattered by violence and displacement.
- Meaning may be fragmented rather than restored.
- Community may be temporary, exhausted, or unable to repair the loss.
- Hope cannot be demanded as a performance.
- Moral response requires truth, protection, justice, memory, and material care.

Failure condition:

The pattern fails if it turns survival into a neat transformation story or uses
spiritual language to avoid public justice and restitution.
