# Ecological Loss No Restoration Test

Type: hard unresolved-suffering and creation-care case study
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A community loses land, species, water safety, farming stability, or ancestral
place through pollution, climate pressure, extraction, or policy failure. Some
mitigation occurs, but the original ecosystem and way of life do not return.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Creation order can be damaged by human greed and neglect.
- Hope must include material repair, policy, restraint, and protection of
  vulnerable communities.
- Ritual or symbolic lament is not enough without changed practice.
- Some losses are irreversible.

Failure condition:

The pattern does not hold if it uses creation language beautifully while
ignoring ecological damage, displacement, and irreversible loss.
