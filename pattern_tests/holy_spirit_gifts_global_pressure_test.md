# Holy Spirit Gifts Global Pressure Test

Type: pneumatology and comparative pressure
Source status: spiritual gifts guardrail

This test asks whether claims about the gifts of the Holy Spirit survive global,
cross-cultural, and interreligious discernment.

## Claim Under Test

If the gifts are part of God, then they should belong to the divine pattern as
signs of the Holy Ghost's work in witness, service, healing, wisdom,
discernment, love, and transformation.

## Pressure Questions

- Does the claimed gift point toward Jesus Christ?
- Does it build up people in love?
- Does it bear fruit over time?
- Does it protect the vulnerable?
- Is it accountable to community discernment?
- Does it respect country, culture, language, and religious background?
- Are rival explanations considered?
- Is the testimony public, ethical, and source-reviewed?

## Comparative Pressure

Other religions also report dreams, visions, healing, prophecy, wisdom, spirit
encounters, and moral transformation. The project must compare respectfully:

- same outward form does not mean same theological source,
- different theological explanations must be named,
- Christian discernment may identify resonance with the Holy Ghost's work,
  but must not erase the other tradition's self-understanding.

## Failure Conditions

The pattern weakens if gifts language is used for:

- spectacle,
- coercion,
- fraud,
- medical irresponsibility,
- superiority over other peoples,
- abuse cover-up,
- false certainty,
- anti-intellectualism,
- ignoring suffering or justice.

## Result Rule

The gifts strengthen the pattern only when they are Jesus-centered,
fruit-bearing, love-governed, accountable, cross-culturally humble, and
consistent with truth and justice.
