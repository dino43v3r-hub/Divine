## Cross-Pattern Failure Conditions

- Image Of God Pattern: weaken wherever dignity is tied to cognition, beauty,
  productivity, tribe, race, caste, gender, citizenship, health, or usefulness.
- Cross And Reversal Pattern: reject wherever cross language asks harmed people
  to remain in danger, hide truth, or forgive without justice.
- Creation-To-Consciousness Pattern: weaken wherever science is used as proof,
  consciousness becomes superiority, animal life is dismissed, or disabled
  persons are ranked.
- Trinity-As-Behavior Pattern: reject wherever Father, Son, and Spirit are
  collapsed into symbols, divided into three gods, or used for spiritual
  control.
- Providence And Contingency Pattern: weaken wherever tragedy is explained too
  neatly, victims are blamed, chance is denied, or hidden divine causes are
  claimed with certainty.
