# Institutional Success Moral Failure Test

Type: Misuse, corruption, and disconfirming pressure
Source status: practical lived source, counterargument

A church grows quickly, gains money, influence, buildings, media reach, and
public reputation. Leaders call this blessing, order, mission, and
transformation. At the same time, staff are exploited, criticism is punished,
victims are silenced, and repentance is replaced by image management.

Pressure on the pattern:

- Visible success is not the same as moral fruit.
- Community language can become manipulation when accountability is absent.
- Transformation must be tested by justice, truth-telling, repair, and care for
  people with less power.
- Trinitarian language is being weaponized if Father, Son, or Holy Spirit are
  used to protect domination.

Failure condition:

The pattern does not hold if it blesses institutional strength while ignoring
abuse, exploitation, coercion, or spiritual bypass.
