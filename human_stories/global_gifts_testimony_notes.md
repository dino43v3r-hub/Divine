# Global Gifts Testimony Notes

Source lane: human stories
Source status: testimony framework

These notes help the project collect testimony about the gifts of the Holy
Spirit across countries, peoples, languages, churches, and religious
backgrounds.

## Testimony Types

- healing and care,
- prophecy and warning,
- dreams and visions,
- tongues and interpretation,
- wisdom and discernment,
- mercy and generosity,
- teaching and encouragement,
- courage under persecution,
- reconciliation across enemies,
- conviction leading to repentance,
- service to the poor and vulnerable.

## Required Context

Each testimony should record:

- country, people, language, and community context when safe,
- whether the source is public or private,
- whether the person could be harmed by exposure,
- church or community discernment,
- long-term fruit,
- possible natural, medical, psychological, social, or cultural explanations,
- whether the story centers Jesus, love, truth, and service.

## Evidence Rule

Testimony can show practical fruit and raise serious theological questions. It
cannot replace source review, medical evidence, community accountability, or
comparative interpretation.

## Failure Rule

A claimed gift weakens the pattern if it produces manipulation, fear,
exploitation, false prophecy, medical negligence, coercive conversion,
spiritual abuse, or contempt for another people.
