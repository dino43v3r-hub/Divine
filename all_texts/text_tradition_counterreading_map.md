# Text-Tradition Counter-Reading Map

Source lane: all texts
Source status: mapped coverage with limits

This file keeps comparative text work honest. Similar motifs across traditions
can generate research questions, but they cannot by themselves prove a
specifically Christian or Trinitarian claim.

## Wisdom Literature

- Pattern signal: order, moral discernment, discipline, mortality, and humility
  recur across many wisdom traditions.
- Counter-reading: wisdom recurrence may reflect human social experience rather
  than shared theological structure.

## Lament And Complaint

- Pattern signal: grief, protest, divine silence, injustice, and hope can be
  voiced rather than suppressed.
- Counter-reading: some laments remain unresolved; forcing resolution falsifies
  the text.

## Epic And Heroic Tradition

- Pattern signal: journey, sacrifice, community threat, and moral testing recur.
- Counter-reading: heroic violence, honor, empire, and conquest may oppose the
  Christian pattern rather than support it.

## Legal And Covenant Texts

- Pattern signal: obligation, justice, community boundaries, repair, and memory
  matter.
- Counter-reading: law can protect the vulnerable or encode hierarchy and
  exclusion.

## Oral Tradition

- Pattern signal: memory, identity, land, ancestor, story, and communal
  formation can be carried orally.
- Counter-reading: extracting motifs without performance context, language, and
  community authority is misuse.

## Analyzer Rule

Comparative recurrence should first be labeled "shared human pattern" or
"research question." It becomes Christian-specific evidence only when linked to
source-specific Christian claims and surviving counter-readings.
