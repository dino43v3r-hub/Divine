# Text Tradition Expansion Notes

Source lane: all texts
Source status: text-tradition coverage expansion

The all-texts lane should include sacred scripture, wisdom literature, epic,
myth, philosophy, law, poetry, ritual, chronicle, oral tradition, and
commentary. Each tradition should be read on its own terms.

Text traditions to add over time:

- Sacred scripture: Bible, Quran, Torah, Veda, Upanishad, Bhagavad Gita, sutra,
  Tripitaka, Avesta, Guru Granth Sahib.
- Wisdom and proverb: moral instruction, elder counsel, practical wisdom,
  household formation, public justice.
- Epic and myth: origin story, hero, exile, return, sacrifice, flood, founding,
  and cosmic order.
- Philosophy and ethics: virtue, reason, good life, suffering, death, justice,
  and metaphysics.
- Law and covenant: command, obligation, judgment, mercy, restoration, public
  order.
- Poetry and lament: grief, desire, praise, protest, memory, and hope.
- Ritual and liturgy: prayer, festival, sacrifice, ceremony, body, repetition,
  and community.
- Chronicle and oral tradition: memory, ancestor, land, migration, conflict,
  repair, and identity.

Counter-reading:

If the pattern appears everywhere, that may show genuine human recurrence, but
it may also show that the categories are too broad. The analyzer should preserve
difference, absence, contradiction, and local meaning.
