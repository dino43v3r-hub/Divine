# Comparative Text Case Notes

Source lane: all texts
Source status: comparative case expansion

This file adds concrete comparative text categories. It is not a claim that all
texts say the same thing.

## Wisdom Literature

Pattern signal:

- Wisdom texts often connect order, instruction, moral consequence, discipline,
  community, and practical flourishing.

Counter-reading:

- Wisdom can become simplistic if it ignores innocent suffering, social power,
  tragedy, or contradiction.

## Lament And Protest

Pattern signal:

- Lament preserves truth when order breaks and meaning is contested.

Counter-reading:

- Lament may not resolve. A pattern that requires quick hope is too weak.

## Epic And Journey

Pattern signal:

- Many epics move through calling, exile, trial, loss, return, and restored or
  transformed identity.

Counter-reading:

- Similar narrative form may reflect human storytelling structure rather than
  theological validation.

## Law And Covenant

Pattern signal:

- Legal and covenantal texts can connect order, obligation, justice, mercy,
  community, and repair.

Counter-reading:

- Law can protect the vulnerable or serve power. Context decides.

## Ritual And Liturgy

Pattern signal:

- Ritual forms memory, identity, body, community, and hope through repeated
  practice.

Counter-reading:

- Ritual can also hide injustice, enforce exclusion, or substitute performance
  for repair.
