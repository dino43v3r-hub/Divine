# Modern Literature Pattern Validation Notes

Source lane: modern literature
Source status: summarized literary comparison, counter-reading

Use modern literature through summaries, criticism notes, public-domain works,
or brief non-substantial references. Do not copy copyrighted novels, poems,
plays, or memoirs into this repository.

Pattern observations:

- Modern novels, short stories, drama, poetry, and memoir often move through
  order, disruption, alienation, desire, moral confrontation, community, loss,
  repair, and possible transformation.
- A character may find meaning without religious belief, may reject inherited
  faith, or may discover that a promised pattern does not fit the actual wound.
- Literature is especially useful because narrative can preserve ambiguity:
  endings may stay unresolved, justice may fail, and hope may remain fragile.
- Counterargument: if the same pattern appears in secular fiction, it may be a
  narrative structure, psychological process, or human meaning-making habit, not
  direct theological evidence.

Validity rule:

Modern literature can test whether the pattern is narratively and humanly
recognizable. It cannot by itself prove divine action. The pattern remains valid
only where it survives ambiguity, rival interpretation, and moral complexity
without forcing the story into a religious conclusion.
