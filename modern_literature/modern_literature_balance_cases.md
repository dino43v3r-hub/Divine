# Modern Literature Balance Cases

Source lane: modern literature
Source status: source-specific balancing note

Modern literature can test whether pattern language survives ambiguity,
secularity, irony, trauma, and unresolved endings.

## Conversion And Doubt Narratives

- Pattern signal: characters may move through longing, crisis, revelation,
  resistance, and moral change.
- Counter-reading: literary conversion may be aesthetic, psychological, social,
  or ironic rather than theological evidence.
- Use rule: summarize themes; do not copy copyrighted text.

## War And Trauma Literature

- Pattern signal: witness, lament, memory, guilt, and fragile hope.
- Counter-reading: many works refuse repair or expose religious language as
  inadequate.
- Use rule: unresolved endings weaken triumphal claims.

## Secular Moral Awakening

- Pattern signal: a person may become truthful, merciful, courageous, or just
  without explicit Christian language.
- Counter-reading: this may support shared human moral pattern rather than
  distinct Trinitarian evidence.
- Use rule: label as broad recurrence unless Christian source links are present.

## Absurdity And Meaninglessness

- Pattern signal: pressure against easy meaning.
- Counter-reading: some modern literature insists that no visible order or
  divine repair appears.
- Use rule: if the literary work resists the pattern, record the resistance.
