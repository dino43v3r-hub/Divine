# Composition Symbol Pressure Notes

Source lane: visual art
Source status: cross-layer synthesis note

Visual art can test whether order, meaning, lament, moral response, and
transformation appear as form rather than only vocabulary.

Observation areas:

- Composition: symmetry, asymmetry, center, margin, rupture, void, crowding,
  movement, perspective, and scale.
- Light and color: illumination, shadow, contrast, hiddenness, tenderness,
  violence, beauty, and ambiguity.
- Gesture and body: open hands, wounds, embrace, turned faces, kneeling,
  resistance, exclusion, and communal presence.
- Sacred image and icon: worship, prayer, mediation, memory, and theological
  restraint.
- Protest art and memorial art: public grief, injustice, power, witness, repair,
  and refusal to forget.

Counter-reading:

Visual beauty does not automatically equal divine presence. Art can reveal
longing, propaganda, domination, grief, hope, or unresolved tension. The pattern
is stronger when it can read both beauty and harm without forcing either into a
simple resolution.
