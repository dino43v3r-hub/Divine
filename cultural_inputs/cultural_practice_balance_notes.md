# Cultural Practice Balance Notes

Source lane: cultural inputs
Source status: source-specific balancing note

The project needs more cultural practice evidence so claims are not only
theological, psychological, or pressure-test driven.

## Health And Care

- Pattern signal: mercy, embodied care, healing, limits, and community support.
- Counter-reading: medicine and care can be explained through science,
  institutions, labor, family, and policy without theological proof.
- Balance rule: include health systems, disability, chronic illness, and
  caregiver burden.

## Economics And Work

- Pattern signal: dignity, provision, stewardship, exploitation, generosity, and
  justice.
- Counter-reading: economic repair requires material change, not only moral
  language.
- Balance rule: include poverty, debt, labor, migration, housing, and
  restitution.

## Education And Formation

- Pattern signal: wisdom, attention, habit, memory, truth, and moral growth.
- Counter-reading: education can also reproduce hierarchy, exclusion, and
  propaganda.
- Balance rule: include access, language, disability, class, and cultural
  context.

## Technology And Attention

- Pattern signal: tool use tests responsibility, truth, power, and community.
- Counter-reading: technology can amplify control, surveillance, addiction, and
  false certainty.
- Balance rule: include AI, media, privacy, labor, and vulnerable communities.
