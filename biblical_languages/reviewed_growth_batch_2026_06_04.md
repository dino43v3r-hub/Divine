| BL-073 | Genesis 1:26-28, Hebrew `tselem`, `demut`, `radah`, `kabash` | Supports image, likeness, vocation, and rule as bounded claims. Counter-reading: royal/ancient Near Eastern background may emphasize delegated rule more than abstract dignity. Pressure: injustice and domination. Boundary: never use image language to excuse coercive rule. |
| BL-074 | Genesis 2:7, Hebrew dust, breath, living creature language | Supports embodied dependence and divine gift. Counter-reading: does not prove later soul/body systems by itself. Pressure: creation-to-consciousness overclaim. Boundary: honor bodies and mortality without ranking persons by cognition. |
| BL-075 | Genesis 4:10, Hebrew blood crying from the ground | Supports injustice as remembered and voiced. Counter-reading: narrative symbolism, not a general metaphysical mechanism. Pressure: suffering without resolution. Boundary: let victim memory interrupt harmony claims. |

Pressure: corruption and punitive misuse. Boundary: dignity claims must protect life rather than sanctify vengeance. |
| BL-077 | Exodus 3:7-8, Hebrew seeing, hearing, knowing, coming down | Supports divine attention to oppression.
