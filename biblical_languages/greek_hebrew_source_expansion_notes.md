# Greek Hebrew Source Expansion Notes

Source lane: biblical Greek and Hebrew
Source status: original-language depth expansion

Original-language study should test translated pattern claims before they become
doctrinal claims.

Greek terms to review:

- logos: word, reason, account, ordering principle; in John, the claim is
  Christological and cannot be reduced to abstract meaning.
- pneuma: spirit, breath, wind; context determines whether the reference is
  human spirit, wind, or Holy Spirit.
- agape: love; do not overstate a single technical meaning without context.
- dikaiosyne: righteousness, justice; moral and covenant dimensions need
  careful reading.
- metanoia: repentance, change of mind, turning; transformation must include
  embodied practice.

Hebrew terms to review:

- ruach: breath, wind, spirit; translation range matters.
- hesed: covenant loyalty, mercy, steadfast love; relational context matters.
- shalom: peace, wholeness, welfare; not merely private calm.
- tsedeq / tsedaqah: righteousness and justice; public and relational order are
  often involved.
- bara, dabar, hokmah, nephesh, lev: creation, word, wisdom, life/self, heart.

Guardrail:

Do not build the pattern from etymology alone. Use lemma, syntax, literary
context, canonical context, translation history, and rival scholarly readings.
