# Reviewed Biblical-Language Growth Batch, 2026-05-27

Source lane: biblical_languages
Source status: reviewed note batch
Reviewed note count: 25

These notes add source-context checks before Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic, or
translation claims are used to strengthen a divine-pattern claim. Each note is
bounded: it supports a question or limited claim, not a proof.

| ID | Source reviewed | Reviewed decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BL-001 | Genesis 1:26-28, Hebrew `tselem` and `demut` | Supports Image Of God as vocation, representation, and dignity in creation context. Counter-reading: royal/ancient Near Eastern image language may mark function more than inner capacity. Practical limit: never rank humans by intelligence or usefulness. |
| BL-002 | Genesis 2:7, Hebrew `neshamah` and `ruach` background | Supports embodied life as gift rather than disembodied spirituality. Counter-reading: breath language is not automatically Trinitarian pneumatology. Practical limit: protect bodily care and creaturely humility. |
| BL-003 | Genesis 9:6, `tselem elohim` after violence | Supports dignity after fall and violence. Counter-reading: legal context concerns bloodshed and justice, not a full anthropology. Practical limit: image language must restrain harm. |
| BL-004 | Psalm 8, Hebrew `kabod`, `hadar`, and human rule | Supports dignity as received honor under God. Counter-reading: dominion can be misused as domination if creation limits are ignored. Practical limit: stewardship before exploitation. |
| BL-005 | Exodus 34:6-7, Hebrew `hesed`, `emet`, `rachum` | Supports covenant mercy joined to faithfulness and justice. Counter-reading: mercy does not cancel accountability in the same passage. Practical limit: repair requires truth. |
| BL-006 | Deuteronomy 6:4-5, `shema`, `echad`, `ahav` | Supports whole-person allegiance and covenant love. Counter-reading: `echad` should not be used as a simple proof text for later Trinitarian doctrine. Practical limit: devotion must become obedience. |
| BL-007 | Leviticus 19:18, Hebrew neighbor-love command | Supports love as public covenant practice. Counter-reading: the immediate context is Israelite communal law and must be read before universalizing. Practical limit: neighbor-love includes justice and restraint. |
| BL-008 | Numbers 6:24-26, `shalom` in priestly blessing | Supports peace as blessing, wholeness, and guarded life. Counter-reading: `shalom` is broader than inner calm and cannot erase conflict. Practical limit: seek social repair, not mere mood. |
| BL-009 | Job 38-42, creation vocabulary under suffering | Supports humility before creation's depth. Counter-reading: divine speeches do not answer every theodicy question. Practical limit: do not use mystery to silence lament. |
| BL-010 | Psalm 51, `ruach`, `lev`, and repentance language | Supports transformation as renewed heart and spirit. Counter-reading: penitential poetry is not a mechanical psychology of change. Practical limit: repentance must include truth and restitution where possible. |
| BL-011 | Isaiah 11:1-9, `ruach YHWH`, wisdom, justice, peace | Supports Spirit, justice, and creation peace as linked prophetic hope. Counter-reading: messianic readings should respect historical prophetic setting. Practical limit: power should serve the vulnerable. |
| BL-012 | Isaiah 53, servant language and suffering | Supports Cross And Reversal only with caution. Counter-reading: Jewish readings may identify the servant differently; Christian use must not erase them. Practical limit: never romanticize victim suffering. |
| BL-013 | Jeremiah 31:31-34, new covenant and `torah` internalized | Supports transformation as covenant renewal. Counter-reading: internal law is not private spirituality severed from community. Practical limit: renewal should produce justice and knowledge of God. |
| BL-014 | Ezekiel 36:26-27, `lev chadash`, `ruach chadashah` | Supports Spirit-enabled obedience. Counter-reading: exile/restoration context matters before individualizing. Practical limit: transformation should repair communal life. |
| BL-015 | Micah 6:8, `mishpat`, `hesed`, humble walking | Supports practical theology as justice, mercy, humility. Counter-reading: the verse is covenant lawsuit rhetoric, not a generic slogan. Practical limit: claims must become public righteousness. |
| BL-016 | John 1:1-18, Greek `logos`, `sarx`, `monogenes` | Supports Logos as personal Christological revelation. Counter-reading: `logos` must not be flattened into abstract reason or pattern. Practical limit: meaning becomes flesh and neighborly presence. |
| BL-017 | Mark 1:15, Greek `metanoeite` and kingdom | Supports repentance as reorientation under God's reign. Counter-reading: not merely private remorse or cognitive change. Practical limit: changed allegiance should produce changed practice. |
| BL-018 | Matthew 5:6, `dikaiosyne` in beatitudes | Supports righteousness/justice desire under the kingdom. Counter-reading: English "righteousness" can hide social justice dimensions. Practical limit: hunger for righteousness must not become moral status-seeking. |
| BL-019 | Luke 4:18-19, Septuagint Isaiah and release vocabulary | Supports Spirit-anointed liberation language. Counter-reading: liberation must be read through Luke's narrative, not imported as a vague political symbol. Practical limit: good news includes the poor and captive. |
| BL-020 | Romans 8, `pneuma`, groaning, adoption, creation | Supports Spirit, suffering, hope, and creation as joined. Counter-reading: groaning means hope is not visible resolution. Practical limit: comfort without denial. |
| BL-021 | 1 Corinthians 12-14, `charismata`, `soma`, `agape` | Supports gifts tested by body-building love. Counter-reading: gifts are not proof of status or spiritual superiority. Practical limit: accountability and intelligibility matter. |
| BL-022 | Philippians 2:5-11, `kenosis` and exaltation | Supports Cross And Reversal through humility and obedience. Counter-reading: debated grammar and hymn form require caution. Practical limit: humility cannot be weaponized against the vulnerable. |
| BL-023 | Galatians 5:22-23, fruit of the Spirit vocabulary | Supports Spirit claims by durable fruit. Counter-reading: virtue lists are not instant tests or personality labels. Practical limit: examine love, patience, self-control over time. |
| BL-024 | Ephesians 4:1-16, unity, gifts, maturity | Supports Trinity-As-Behavior as ecclesial formation. Counter-reading: unity cannot mean enforced sameness. Practical limit: truth in love must protect the body. |
| BL-025 | Revelation 21-22, new creation imagery | Supports hope as creation restored, not escaped. Counter-reading: apocalyptic imagery must not become date-setting or triumphal prediction. Practical limit: hope should sustain repair and worship. |
