# Source-Specific Lexeme Review Notes

Source lane: biblical Greek and Hebrew
Source status: lexeme review expansion

This file makes the biblical-language lane more concrete. Each lexeme must be
checked in context; no pattern should be built from word roots alone.

## Hebrew Review

- ruach: wind, breath, spirit. Review creation, prophetic, wisdom, and renewal
  contexts before connecting it to Spirit language.
- hesed: covenant loyalty, steadfast love, mercy. Review narrative and covenant
  usage; do not reduce it to sentiment.
- shalom: peace, wholeness, welfare, restored order. Include social and public
  dimensions, not merely private calm.
- tsedeq / tsedaqah: righteousness and justice. Track courtroom, covenant,
  royal, and communal settings.
- dabar: word, matter, event. Avoid simple equivalence with Greek logos.
- hokmah: wisdom, skill, order, practical discernment. Review Proverbs, Job,
  and wisdom tradition tensions.

## Greek Review

- logos: word, account, reason. In John, review literary and Christological
  context before using broad philosophical parallels.
- pneuma: wind, breath, spirit. Context decides whether human spirit, wind,
  unclean spirit, or Holy Spirit is meant.
- agape: love. Do not claim a single technical meaning in every context.
- dikaiosyne: righteousness and justice. Review Pauline, Matthean, and
  Septuagint backgrounds.
- metanoia: repentance, turning, change of mind. Transformation must include
  practice and allegiance, not only inner feeling.
- koinonia: sharing, participation, communion. Review economic, ecclesial, and
  sacramental dimensions carefully.

## Failure Conditions

The language layer fails if it:

- uses etymology as proof
- ignores syntax and genre
- treats translation as transparent
- collapses Hebrew and Greek terms into one English concept
- makes doctrine from isolated word matches
