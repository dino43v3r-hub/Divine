Title: Symphony In Light And Shadow
Genre: Classical / Symphony
Type: Original analysis seed

Movement I: Order
The strings begin with a clear rising theme.
Woodwinds answer with a gentler phrase, and the brass holds the structure firm.
The music feels like a city waking into form.

Movement II: Lament
The tempo slows.
Cellos carry a minor melody over quiet timpani.
The theme bends downward, as if memory has become grief.

Movement III: Struggle
Rhythm returns in sharp fragments.
The orchestra argues with itself, breaking the theme into conflict and motion.
The music searches for a path through pressure.

Movement IV: Transformation
The first theme returns, changed by the journey.
Strings, brass, and choir-like harmony rise together.
The ending does not erase sorrow; it gathers it into hope.
