Title: Quantum And Science Source Framework
Area: Quantum And Science Claims
Source Types: Physicist/Primary Science, Peer-Reviewed/Academic, Philosophy Of Science, Counterargument

Quantum physics involves experiment, measurement, probability, uncertainty, mathematical structure, wavefunction models, and interpretation.
Any claim about quantum mechanics must be tied to physicist-authored sources, primary science, peer review, journal research, data, equation-level clarity, and evidence.

Philosophy of science should distinguish model, interpretation, causality, realism, epistemology, and probability.
Science can support disciplined wonder about an ordered but non-simplistic world, but it should not be used as vague proof of God.

Counterargument: quantum uncertainty does not automatically imply divine action, consciousness, prayer effects, or theological proof.
Alternative explanation: quantum theory may describe physical systems without making metaphysical conclusions.
Limitation: use quantum language only as a humility and complexity guardrail unless stronger sources support a more specific claim.
