# Science Counterarguments And Limits

Area: Quantum And Science Claims
Source Types: Physicist/Primary Science, Peer-Reviewed/Academic, Philosophy Of Science, Counterargument
Source status: source reviewed

This file records objections that should be kept visible when the analyzer finds
science-related pattern signals.

## Counterarguments

1. A lawful universe does not automatically prove Christian doctrine.
2. Mathematical structure can be interpreted through realism, instrumentalism,
   Platonism, naturalism, theism, or other metaphysical frameworks.
3. Biological emergence and consciousness do not by themselves prove a divine
   plan.
4. Quantum probability does not justify vague claims about prayer, miracles,
   consciousness, or moral freedom.
5. Fine-tuning arguments require careful probability assumptions and competing
   explanations.
6. Scientific models are powerful but limited; they answer some questions while
   leaving metaphysical and theological questions open.

## Responsible Pattern Use

The strongest science-related pattern is modest:

Physical order, mathematical intelligibility, probability, emergence,
consciousness, moral agency, and worship can be compared as layered features of
human experience and inquiry.

The pattern should be called a research hypothesis, not a proof. It becomes more
credible only when it names limits, alternatives, and source quality.
