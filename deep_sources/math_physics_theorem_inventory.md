# Math And Physics Theorem Inventory For Pattern Testing

Source lane: deep sources
Source status: theorem/principle inventory

These theorems and principles can help test congruence. They should be used as
discipline, not as shortcuts to proof.

## Mathematics

### Pythagorean Theorem

- Responsible use: geometric relation, measurement, structure, and ratio.
- Pattern use: supports the modest claim that some spatial relations are
  intelligible and stable.
- Limit: does not prove metaphysical or theological claims.

### Prime Numbers And Fundamental Theorem Of Arithmetic

- Responsible use: factorization, discreteness, structure, and uniqueness.
- Pattern use: supports wonder about order within number systems.
- Limit: formal arithmetic structure is not the same as divine action.

### Symmetry And Group Theory

- Responsible use: invariance, transformation, conservation, classification,
  and physics modeling.
- Pattern use: supports careful language about order through relation and
  transformation.
- Limit: symmetry is not automatically beauty, goodness, or Trinity.

### Godel Incompleteness

- Responsible use: limits of sufficiently strong formal systems.
- Pattern use: supports humility about formalization.
- Limit: does not prove God, mystery, or theology. It only sets limits inside
  formal mathematical systems.

## Statistics And Probability

### Bayes' Theorem

- Responsible use: updating confidence from prior probability and likelihood.
- Pattern use: helps the project update confidence gradually as evidence grows.
- Limit: depends on priors and likelihood assumptions; it cannot rescue weak
  evidence.

### Law Of Large Numbers

- Responsible use: long-run stability of averages under defined conditions.
- Pattern use: reminds the project that small samples are fragile.
- Limit: does not apply when sources are curated, biased, or non-random.

### Regression / Correlation Limits

- Responsible use: relationships among variables and predictive patterns.
- Pattern use: helps separate association from explanation.
- Limit: correlation does not establish causation or theology.

## Physics

### Conservation Laws

- Responsible use: energy, momentum, charge, and symmetry-linked invariants in
  physical systems.
- Pattern use: supports disciplined attention to stability and constraint.
- Limit: conservation laws do not imply moral or spiritual conservation.

### Second Law Of Thermodynamics

- Responsible use: entropy and directionality in thermodynamic systems.
- Pattern use: can support modest discussion of physical irreversibility and
  limits.
- Limit: should not be used as a vague metaphor for sin, decline, or culture.

### Relativity

- Responsible use: spacetime, reference frames, gravity, and limits on signal
  propagation.
- Pattern use: supports humility about observer perspective and physical scale.
- Limit: "relative" in physics is not moral relativism.

### Quantum Uncertainty

- Responsible use: non-commuting observables and probabilistic prediction.
- Pattern use: supports disciplined humility about physical prediction.
- Limit: does not prove free will, miracles, prayer, consciousness, or God.

### No-Signaling And Entanglement Limits

- Responsible use: entanglement correlations do not permit faster-than-light
  communication.
- Pattern use: blocks mystical overclaims about instant causal influence.
- Limit: entanglement is not a metaphorical license for all forms of connection.

## Logic

### Modus Ponens / Modus Tollens

- Responsible use: valid inference forms.
- Pattern use: helps test whether conclusions actually follow.
- Limit: validity does not guarantee true premises.

### Non-Contradiction

- Responsible use: a claim and its denial cannot both be true in the same sense
  at the same time.
- Pattern use: forces the project to clarify terms and avoid equivocation.
- Limit: paradox, analogy, and mystery require careful theological handling, not
  sloppy contradiction.

## Use Rule

Every theorem or principle should be logged with:

- what it formally says,
- what source area supports it,
- what claim it permits,
- what claim it blocks,
- which scale or category boundary applies.
