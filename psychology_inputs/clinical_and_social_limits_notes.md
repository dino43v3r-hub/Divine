# Clinical And Social Limits Notes

Source lane: psychology and human behavior
Source status: psychology limit expansion

Psychology helps the project ask whether transformation language describes
actual human processes. It also sets limits on theological overclaim.

## Trauma

Pattern signal:

- Safety, memory, body, trust, grief, and repeated care matter for repair.

Limit:

- Trauma recovery is not linear. Religious pressure to forgive or find meaning
  can deepen harm.

## Attachment

Pattern signal:

- Belonging, secure care, rupture, repair, and reliable presence shape human
  transformation.

Limit:

- Attachment theory can illuminate spiritual life, but it does not prove a
  doctrine of God.

## Habit Formation

Pattern signal:

- Repeated practices shape attention, desire, identity, and moral action.

Limit:

- Habit change may be psychological, social, economic, medical, or spiritual.
  Do not collapse causes.

## Moral Injury

Pattern signal:

- Guilt, shame, betrayal, truth-telling, confession, justice, and repair are
  central to moral transformation.

Limit:

- Some injuries require public accountability and material repair, not private
  catharsis.

## Forgiveness

Pattern signal:

- Forgiveness can be part of repair, reconciliation, and freedom from vengeance.

Limit:

- Forgiveness must not be coerced. Safety and justice come before restored
  relationship.
