# Trauma Attachment Repair Notes

Source lane: psychology and human behavior
Source status: psychology synthesis note

Psychology can help test whether the pattern describes actual human processes
without reducing theology to mechanism.

Observation areas:

- Attachment: trust, rupture, repair, belonging, abandonment, and secure care.
- Trauma: silence, intrusive memory, shame, body response, hypervigilance,
  dissociation, grief, and slow recovery.
- Habit and practice: repeated action, attention, community reinforcement,
  relapse, discipline, and change over time.
- Meaning-making: narrative identity, interpretation, moral injury, forgiveness,
  justice, and hope.
- Social support: counseling, friendship, mutual aid, recovery groups, pastoral
  care, and institutional responsibility.

Counter-reading:

Transformation may have psychological, social, medical, and economic causes. A
Christian interpretation should not deny those causes. The pattern is useful
only if it helps people pursue truth, care, justice, and repair without forcing
premature closure.
