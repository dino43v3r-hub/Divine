# Source-Specific Psychology Limits

Source lane: psychology and human behavior
Source status: cross-layer limit notes

These notes keep psychology from being used as disguised proof. Psychological
findings can illuminate coping, attachment, forgiveness, ritual, identity, and
transformation, but they do not prove divine causation.

## Trauma And Attachment

- Pattern signal: secure attachment, trust, embodiment, and relational repair
  can shape religious experience and community behavior.
- Counter-reading: trauma can also make religious language frightening,
  coercive, or dissociative.
- Allowed use: psychology can clarify vulnerability and care needs.
- Forbidden use: do not infer that suffering was spiritually beneficial.

## Forgiveness Research

- Pattern signal: forgiveness can relate to stress, coping, meaning, and moral
  repair.
- Counter-reading: forgiveness language can be weaponized against victims when
  justice, safety, and truth are absent.
- Allowed use: study forgiveness as one possible process.
- Forbidden use: do not make forgiveness a requirement for healing or evidence
  of faithfulness.

## Ritual And Habit

- Pattern signal: repeated worship and communal practice can form attention,
  memory, identity, and action.
- Counter-reading: rituals can also normalize exclusion, avoidance, abuse, or
  authoritarian control.
- Allowed use: connect practice with formation cautiously.
- Forbidden use: do not assume all religious formation is healthy.

## Moral Injury

- Pattern signal: guilt, betrayal, institutional failure, confession, and repair
  are central to moral life.
- Counter-reading: some moral injuries remain unresolved because institutions
  never confess or repair harm.
- Allowed use: moral injury can pressure-test repentance and reconciliation.
- Forbidden use: do not call unresolved guilt or betrayal "growth" without
  repair.
