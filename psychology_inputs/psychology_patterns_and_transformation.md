# Psychology: Human Process And Transformation

Source lane: psychology and other texts

Psychology can help the app detect human processes beneath the words: perception,
attention, memory, emotion, attachment, trauma, habit, desire, identity,
motivation, and repair.

Pattern observation:

- People often repeat patterns before they can name them.
- Trauma and grief can shape attention, trust, memory, and expectation.
- Desire and attachment influence what people call good, safe, beautiful, or
  threatening.
- Habits and embodied practices can turn insight into transformation.
- Psychology can illuminate a spiritual pattern without reducing theology to a
  mental mechanism.

Cross-layer question:
