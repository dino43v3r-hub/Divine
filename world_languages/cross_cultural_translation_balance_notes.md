# Cross-Cultural Translation Balance Notes

Source lane: world languages
Source status: source-specific balancing note

Global claims need actual translation and language notes. This file adds
balancing questions for world-language evidence.

## Spirit / Breath / Life Terms

- Research need: compare how different languages express breath, life, spirit,
  wind, soul, and divine presence.
- Pattern use: possible resonance with Spirit language.
- Counter-reading: similar metaphors may arise from embodied human experience,
  not shared doctrine.

## Word / Wisdom / Meaning Terms

- Research need: compare terms used for word, reason, wisdom, teaching, way,
  law, and truth.
- Pattern use: possible bridge to Logos and wisdom themes.
- Counter-reading: local philosophical categories may not map onto Greek
  `logos` or Christian Christology.

## Mercy / Justice / Repair Terms

- Research need: compare terms for mercy, justice, forgiveness, restoration,
  shame, honor, reconciliation, and restitution.
- Pattern use: moral repair can be tracked cross-culturally.
- Counter-reading: forgiveness and repair may be social, legal, ritual, or
  economic rather than individual feeling.

## Translation Guardrail

Before using a non-English source as evidence, record:

- original term,
- literal range,
- translator choice,
- community context,
- genre,
- rival translation,
- whether Christian vocabulary was introduced by mission, colonial history, or
  local theological development.
