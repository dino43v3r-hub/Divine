# Language Family Expansion Notes

Source lane: world languages
Source status: language-family coverage expansion

The project should expand language-family and text-tradition coverage before
calling any pattern universal.

Coverage priorities:

- Afro-Asiatic: Hebrew, Arabic, Aramaic, Coptic, Amharic, Akkadian.
- Indo-European: Greek, Latin, Sanskrit, Persian, English, Spanish, German,
  Russian, Hindi.
- Sino-Tibetan: Classical Chinese, Mandarin, Tibetan, Burmese.
- Niger-Congo: Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Zulu, Xhosa, Akan.
- Austronesian: Malay, Indonesian, Tagalog, Hawaiian, Maori.
- Dravidian: Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam.
- Indigenous Americas: Nahuatl, Maya, Quechua, Aymara, Navajo, Cherokee, Cree,
  Ojibwe, Mapuche.
- Australian and Papuan traditions: include oral tradition, land, memory,
  ancestor, ritual, and translation caution.

Method:

Track original term, translation range, metaphor, grammar, genre, speaker,
community, colonial context, and rival interpretation. A translated word such as
order, spirit, wisdom, law, heart, breath, soul, justice, or peace may not map
cleanly across languages.

Validity rule:

Broad recurrence across language families can suggest shared human concerns. It
does not permit a universal claim until actual source notes and counter-readings
exist across many families and traditions.
