# World Languages: Translation, Semantics, And Culture

Source lane: world languages notes

World-language comparison helps the app see that meaning is not only a repeated
English word. Meaning can shift through grammar, idiom, metaphor, semantic range,
word order, culture, and context.

Pattern observation:

- Translation can reveal a theme, but it can also hide ambiguity.
- Metaphor may carry communal memory that a literal gloss misses.
- Grammar can show agency, relation, command, desire, time, or possibility.
- A pattern becomes stronger when it survives careful translation instead of
  depending on one convenient word.
