# Divine Pattern Claim Ledger

Type: claim control ledger
Source status: active review ledger

This ledger keeps the project from speaking with one level of certainty about
different kinds of claims. It separates source-supported observations from
theological interpretation, discernment, analogy, and practical use.

## Status Key

- `reviewed_evidence`: enough source-specific support exists for cautious use.
- `developing_evidence`: sources exist, but more review or balance is needed.
- `theological_interpretation`: a Christian reading of the evidence, not an
  independent empirical proof.
- `discernment_question`: useful for prayer, pastoral reflection, and communal
  testing, but not yet a conclusion.
- `practical_theology_use`: safe as a disciplined practice guide when used with
  humility and accountability.
- `analogy_only`: illuminating comparison that must not be promoted to proof.
- `research_question_only`: mapped idea that needs sources and counter-readings.
- `weakened_or_limited`: pressure tests require the claim to be narrowed.

## Promotion Rule

No claim moves to `reviewed_evidence` unless it has:

- source-specific notes,
- at least one counter-reading,
- lane balance checks,
- a pressure-test result,
- cautious confidence language,
- a practical-theology boundary when applied to daily life.
