# Source Lane Balance Targets

Type: source coverage policy
Source status: active target map

The project should not grow only where collection is easiest. A disciplined
theological assistant needs a balanced body of sources: scripture and
theologians, languages and history, art and culture, psychology and lived
stories, other religious traditions, pressure tests, and methodological
guardrails.

## Target Bands

Targets are not final truth. They are working coverage goals for source notes
and reviewed source packs. A lane can exceed its target only if thin lanes are
also growing.

| Lane | Minimum | Healthy target | Review cap | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| biblical_languages | 12 | 30 | 60 | Check Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic, syntax, translation range, and covenant language. |
| world_languages | 12 | 30 | 60 | Test translation, metaphor, oral tradition, and language-family breadth. |
| all_texts | 20 | 50 | 90 | Compare sacred, wisdom, legal, poetic, ritual, oral, and philosophical texts. |
| other_religious_texts | 20 | 50 | 90 | Study other traditions respectfully, with real difference preserved. |
| theologians | 30 | 75 | 120 | Deepen doctrine with primary texts and disagreements across eras. |
| history_inputs | 20 | 50 | 90 | Test the pattern against power, memory, conflict, reform, and consequences. |
| visual_art | 20 | 50 | 90 | Read image, form, gesture, beauty, lament, glory, and iconography. |
| psychology_inputs | 20 | 50 | 90 | Test perception, trauma, attachment, habit, desire, identity, and repair. |
| human_stories | 20 | 50 | 90 | Keep practical theology grounded in lived grief, repair, vocation, and community. |
| cultural_inputs | 20 | 50 | 90 | Apply the pattern to politics, economics, technology, ecology, health, work, and education. |
| modern_literature | 12 | 30 | 60 | Test recurrence and limits through narrative, memoir, drama, and poetry summaries. |
| deep_sources | 20 | 50 | 90 | Hold math, statistics, science, suffering, and counterargument guardrails. |
| pattern_tests | 20 | 45 | 75 | Pressure-test claims without letting pressure tests become the whole project. |
| research_documents | 20 | 45 | 75 | Maintain method, cloud review, claim rules, ledgers, and synthesis policies. |

## Balance Rule

Before strengthening a broad claim, check whether its supporting lanes are
healthy. If one lane is above cap while a directly relevant lane is below
minimum, the claim stays limited.

Examples:

- A Greek or Hebrew claim cannot strengthen without biblical-language notes.
- A global claim cannot strengthen without world-language and other-religious
  source notes.
- A practical claim cannot strengthen without human stories, psychology,
  pastoral theology, and pressure tests.
- A science claim cannot strengthen without deep-source guardrails and
  counterarguments.
