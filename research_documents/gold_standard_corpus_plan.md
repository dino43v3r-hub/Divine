# Gold Standard Corpus Plan

Type: research operations plan
Source status: human review queue

This plan creates a smaller, higher-trust corpus that can discipline the larger
candidate collection. The purpose is not to reduce the project's imagination.
It is to give every major claim a reviewed anchor set before broad source volume
raises confidence.

## Rule

No major Divine Pattern claim should be treated as mature because many candidate
sources repeat a signal. A claim matures only when a small set of high-quality
sources has been reviewed directly, with counter-readings and failure conditions
recorded.

## Target Size

Build a corpus of 25 to 40 reviewed sources.

Each source entry should include:

- bibliographic identity,
- primary-source or qualified-secondary-source status,
- reviewed passage or section,
- evidence supported by the source,
- Christian interpretation being proposed,
- discernment question still open,
- analogy limit,
- rival explanation or counter-reading,
- failure condition,
- practical-theology boundary,
- confidence effect.

## Core Lanes

### Scripture And Biblical Languages

Minimum target: 6 to 8 entries.

Include creation, wisdom/Logos, lament, incarnation, cross, resurrection, Spirit,
new creation, and original-language notes where they affect interpretation.

Required counter-readings:

- Jewish interpretation where relevant,
- historical-critical readings,
- genre and canonical-context cautions.

### Historical And Theological Witness

Minimum target: 5 to 7 entries.

Include patristic, medieval, Reformation, modern, and global church voices.

Required counter-readings:

- disagreement within Christian tradition,
- misuse history,
- doctrinal boundary checks.

### Suffering, Abuse, And Failed Repair

Minimum target: 4 to 6 entries.

Include unresolved suffering, moral injury, spiritual abuse, institutional
failure, poverty, disability, war, grief, and cases where repair does not arrive
inside the visible story.

Required counter-readings:

- claims that later good does not undo harm,
- victim-protection concerns,
- spiritual-bypass risks.

### Comparative Religion And Culture

Minimum target: 4 to 6 entries.

Include sources that can be read on their own terms before Christian comparison.

Required counter-readings:

- internal interpretations from the tradition being studied,
- human cognition, ritual, social formation, and historical-contact accounts,
- warnings against flattening difference.

### Science, Mathematics, And Philosophy

Minimum target: 4 to 6 entries.

Include philosophy of science, probability/statistics, mathematics, physics, AI
pattern recognition, neuroscience, or biology only where source scope is clear.

Required counter-readings:

- naturalistic explanations,
- scale and domain limits,
- analogy-only boundaries,
- technical expertise checks.

### Practical Theology And Human Stories

Minimum target: 3 to 5 entries.

Include pastoral theology, spiritual formation, community repair, testimony, and
lived experience with explicit harm safeguards.

Required counter-readings:

- coercion risk,
- group pressure,
- trauma and psychology limits,
- practical fruit failure.

## Promotion Gate

A source from this corpus may affect a claim only after all eight promotion
fields are filled:

- evidence,
- interpretation,
- discernment,
- analogy,
- practical use,
- counter-reading,
- failure condition,
- machine-label boundary.

If any field is missing, the source remains `developing_evidence` or
`candidate_lead`.
