## Human-Review Separation

Every promoted item must separate:

- evidence: what the reviewed source actually supports,
- interpretation: the theological reading being proposed,
- discernment: what still needs communal, prayerful, and accountable testing,
- analogy: what illuminates without proving,
- practical use: what the claim asks people to do,
- failure condition: what would weaken, revise, or reject the claim.

Automated labels route attention only. They never settle truth.
