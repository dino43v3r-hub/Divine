# Layer Balance Policy

Type: source coverage policy
Source status: active balancing rule

The project should not let one or two lanes dominate the evidence map. Pressure
tests, human stories, psychology, and theologians are essential, but the divine
pattern becomes distorted if biblical languages, world languages, modern
literature, cultural practice, visual art, history, all-texts, and general
research method remain thin.

## Current Balance Concern

Recent layer counts showed pressure tests, human stories, psychology, and
theologians far ahead of several source-specific lanes. This creates two risks:

- the project becomes mostly a pressure-testing machine without enough primary
  source depth,
- broad claims are strengthened before language, culture, literature, and
  biblical-language evidence is broad enough.

## Target Rule

Until the thin lanes catch up, new collection and manual source notes should
prioritize:

- biblical_languages,
- world_languages,
- modern_literature,
- cultural_inputs,
- research_documents,
- all_texts,
- visual_art,
- history_inputs,
- deep_sources.

## Routing Rule

Do not route ordinary theology, history, culture, psychology, or testimony
sources into `pattern_tests` automatically. Route to `pattern_tests` only when
the source is primarily about pressure, failure, abuse, contradiction,
counterargument, unresolved suffering, science overclaim, or discernment risk.

## Promotion Rule

A claim cannot be promoted to strong evidence if its supporting layers are
unbalanced. It must have at least one source-specific note from the relevant
underrepresented lane, or it stays at `limited_christian_signal`,
`shared_human_pattern`, or `research_question_only`.

## Practical Next Steps

- Add biblical-language notes before using Hebrew/Greek terms as support.
- Add world-language examples before making global claims.
- Add modern literature and all-texts notes before claiming broad human
  recurrence.
- Add cultural-input notes before using politics, economics, health, ecology, or
  technology as pattern evidence.
- Add general research-method notes before treating cloud reference metadata as
  evidence.
