# Practical Theology Pattern Use

Type: practical theology guide
Source status: active use guide

The Divine project should not only identify patterns. It should help people
engage faithfully with God, neighbor, self, creation, and community. Practical
theology asks what the pattern does in lived experience.

## Core Practice Loop

Notice -> Name -> Discern -> Practice -> Review

Notice: attend to what is actually happening before explaining it. Look for
order, beauty, disorder, pain, longing, conflict, gift, responsibility, or hope.

Name: describe the situation truthfully. Is this creation, lament, temptation,
injustice, vocation, worship, reconciliation, repair, or waiting?

Discern: ask how scripture, prayer, conscience, community, wisdom, and pressure
tests shape the faithful response. Separate evidence from interpretation.

Practice: choose a small embodied act. Pray, repent, reconcile, rest, serve,
study, tell the truth, protect the vulnerable, create beauty, or seek counsel.

Review: look for fruit over time. Does the response produce love, joy, peace,
patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control, justice,
truth, humility, and neighbor-love?

## Day-To-Day Uses

Family conflict: notice the wound, name the truth without exaggeration, discern
whether repentance or boundaries are needed, practice one concrete act of
truthful love, review whether repair became more honest.

Work and vocation: notice the system, name the pressure, discern whether dignity
and justice are protected, practice faithful excellence and courage, review
whether work serves people rather than using them.

Grief and unresolved suffering: notice the pain without rushing it, name lament,
discern the next faithful act, practice presence and care, review whether hope
remains truthful rather than forced.

Spiritual gifts: notice the gift or claim, name its fruit, discern with
scripture and community, practice service rather than status, review whether it
builds up others in love.

Other religious encounters: notice the person and tradition, name shared human
longing and real difference, discern humbly, practice respectful witness and
neighbor-love, review whether the encounter produced truth and charity.

Pattern perception: notice the recurrence, name possible explanations, discern
with evidence and counterarguments, practice caution, review whether the pattern
still holds under pressure.

## Misuse Warnings

Do not use the divine pattern to control people, silence grief, excuse
injustice, predict private outcomes, bypass professional care, or claim proof
where only analogy or discernment exists.
