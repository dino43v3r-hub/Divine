# Divine Pattern Finder Design

This note defines the first working model for finding divine-pattern candidates in the research corpus.

## Core Model

The tool looks for layered convergence:

Physical Order -> Mathematical Structure -> Mathematical Theophany -> Quantum Probability -> Life And Consciousness -> Meaning And Logos -> Moral Response -> Worship And Community -> Transformation

This is not a proof engine. It is a hypothesis finder.

## Layer Definitions

### Physical Order

Looks for language about creation, physical law, order, providence, cosmos, universe, causality, and nature.

### Mathematical Structure

Looks for mathematics, number, geometry, equation, logic, probability, prediction, pattern, and intelligibility.

### Quantum Probability

Looks for quantum mechanics, uncertainty, wavefunction, measurement, probability, particles, superposition, and entanglement.

### Life And Consciousness

Looks for life, biology, consciousness, mind, brain, human beings, image of God, soul, and breath.

### Meaning And Logos

Looks for meaning, Logos, Word, wisdom, truth, revelation, reason, knowledge, and purpose.

### Moral Response

Looks for morality, sin, justice, mercy, righteousness, repentance, forgiveness, obedience, and virtue.

### Worship And Community

Looks for worship, prayer, ritual, church, community, sacrament, baptism, Eucharist, faith, and discipleship.

### Transformation

Looks for transformation, sanctification, Holy Spirit, grace, salvation, redemption, resurrection, new life, and hope.

## Responsible Interpretation

If every layer appears, the tool can say that the corpus supports a layered-convergence hypothesis.

It must not say that the pattern proves God.

The proper claim is:

AI can help compare these layers and generate research hypotheses about whether order, intelligibility, moral transformation, and worship form a coherent Christian pattern across reality and human life.
