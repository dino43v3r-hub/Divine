 | notes      = The '''Didache''' [[w:Koine Greek|Koine Greek]]: {{polytonic|Διδαχή}}, ''Didachē'', meaning "Teaching"... is the common name of a brief [[w:Early Christianity|early Christian]] [[w:treatise|treatise]] (dated to any point in the first three Christian centuries), containing instructions for Christian communities. The text is possibly the first written [[w:catechism|catechism]], with three main sections dealing with Christian lessons, rituals such as [[w:baptism|baptism]] and [[w:eucharist|eucharist]], and Church organization. It was considered by some of the [[w:Church Fathers|Church Fathers]] as part of the [[w:New Testament|New Testament]] but rejected as [[w:Apocrypha#Spurious writings|spurious]] by others, eventually not accepted into the [[w:Biblical canon#Orthodox.2C Catholic and Protestant|New Testament canon]] with the exception of the [[w:Ethiopian Orthodox Church|Ethiopian Orthodox Church]] "broader canon". The [[w:Roman Catholic Church|Roman Catholic Church]] has accepted it as part of the collection of [[w:Apostolic Fathers|Apostolic Fathers]].
}}
