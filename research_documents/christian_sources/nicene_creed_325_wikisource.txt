 | notes = The '''Nicene Creed''' is a concise summary of the core beliefs of Christianity, primarily concerned with defining the nature of the three persons comprising the [[w:Trinity|Trinity]] - God, Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit. It was adopted by the [[Portal:First Council of Nicaea|First Ecumenical Council]], held in Nicaea in 325. The term is also loosely but commonly applied to a longer creed traditionally attributed to the [[Portal:First Council of Constantinople|Second Ecumenical Council]], held in Constantinople in 381 but of which there is evidence only from 451 onward. Because of its supposed origin and because it has been considered to be an elaboration of the Creed of Nicaea, this longer creed is distinguished from the original Nicene Creed by being called the [[Niceno-Constantinopolitan Creed]].
