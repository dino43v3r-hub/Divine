# Divine Pattern Hypothesis Test Framework

This framework turns the project into a structured research test.

## Central Hypothesis

Within Christianity, God is understood as the source of being and moral order; Jesus Christ as the embodied revelation and redemptive pattern; and the Holy Spirit as the active divine presence who transforms persons and communities.

The broader divine-pattern hypothesis asks whether reality and human life show layered intelligibility:

Being -> Order -> Life -> Consciousness -> Meaning -> Moral Response -> Transformation.

## Test Domains

### 1. Scripture And Early Christian Logos

Question:

How do scripture and early Christian interpretation connect Logos, creation, wisdom, providence, Christ, and the intelligibility of reality?

Evidence to add:

- Gospel of John
- Genesis creation texts
- Pauline Christology
- Proverbs/Wisdom traditions
- early church fathers on Logos and creation

### 2. Historical Theology

Question:

How have Christian theologians described creation, natural law, providence, Trinity, incarnation, and sanctification?

Evidence to add:

- Augustine
- Aquinas
- Athanasius
- Irenaeus
- Cappadocian Fathers
- Reformation and modern theology where relevant

### 3. Philosophy Of Science

Question:

What are laws of nature, explanation, causality, realism, probability, and scientific intelligibility?

Evidence to add:

- philosophy of laws of nature
- scientific realism and anti-realism
- causality
- explanation
- induction
- emergence

### 4. Philosophy Of Mathematics

Question:

Why is mathematics so effective in describing physical reality, and what does that imply or not imply?

Evidence to add:

- indispensability arguments
- mathematical realism and nominalism
- applicability of mathematics
- structure and prediction

### 5. Physics And Quantum Mechanics

Question:

How do physicists actually understand law, probability, measurement, uncertainty, fields, particles, spacetime, and cosmology?

Evidence to add:

- classical mechanics
- relativity
- quantum mechanics
- statistical mechanics
- cosmology
- physicist-authored explanations, not vague popular mysticism

### 6. Cognitive Science

Question:

How do humans detect patterns, construct meaning, form beliefs, reason symbolically, and experience transcendence?

Evidence to add:

- pattern recognition
- predictive processing
- cognition and belief formation
- meaning-making
- consciousness
- social cognition

### 7. Anthropology And Psychology Of Worship

Question:

How do worship, ritual, morality, community, identity, and transformation shape behavior across cultures?

Evidence to add:

- ritual studies
- anthropology of religion
- moral psychology
- prosocial behavior
- social bonding
- spiritual formation

## Evidence Standard

For each domain, classify claims as:

- source-backed finding
- plausible hypothesis
- speculative claim
- unsupported claim

The project should not say "science proves God."

The stronger claim is:

Across disciplines, there may be a recurring layered pattern of intelligibility, order, meaning, moral response, and transformation that Christian theology interprets through creation, Logos, Christ, Spirit, and providence.
