# Weakened Claims Register

Type: pattern revision log
Source status: active weakening register

This register names claims that must stay weakened until further source review,
pressure testing, and counter-readings justify a narrower version.

## Weakened Claim: Pattern Recurrence Proves Divine Origin

- Status: `weakened_by_pressure`
- Reason: recurrence can result from selection bias, shared human cognition,
  social formation, broad religious motifs, or curated sources.
- Narrow allowed claim: recurrence can generate research questions and modest
  confidence updates after statistical and comparison checks.

## Weakened Claim: Quantum Physics Supports Prayer Or Consciousness Claims

- Status: `do_not_strengthen_claim`
- Reason: quantum concepts are often misused outside their technical scale.
- Narrow allowed claim: quantum theory supports humility about physical
  prediction and requires qualified sources.

## Weakened Claim: Other Religions Secretly Mean The Same Thing

- Status: `do_not_strengthen_claim`
- Reason: this erases traditions' self-understanding and collapses differences.
- Narrow allowed claim: Christians may discern resonance or possible witness,
  while preserving each tradition's own account.

## Weakened Claim: Holy Ghost Gifts Are Valid Because They Are Spectacular

- Status: `weakened_by_pressure`
- Reason: spectacle can be produced by fraud, charisma, trauma, group pressure,
  or misinterpretation.
- Narrow allowed claim: gifts may strengthen the pattern only where they are
  Christ-centered, fruit-bearing, accountable, loving, truthful, and just.

## Weakened Claim: Suffering Is Redeemed Because Later Good Appears

- Status: `weakened_by_pressure`
- Reason: later resilience or witness does not undo absent repair.
- Narrow allowed claim: faithful response may include lament, witness, care,
  justice, and hope without claiming completed repair.

## Weakened Claim: Language-Family And Text-Tradition Coverage Is Universal

- Status: `mapped_not_universal`
- Reason: mapped categories are not source-specific coverage.
- Narrow allowed claim: the project has a research map for future sampling.
