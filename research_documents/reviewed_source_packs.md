# Reviewed Source Packs

Type: reviewed evidence structure
Source status: active source-pack template

Reviewed source packs keep the Divine project from relying on scattered
references. Each pack ties a claim to primary sources, interpreters, opposing
readings, pressure tests, and practical-theology use.

## Pack 1: Biblical Typology And Christ Fulfillment

Claim boundary: Christians may read certain earlier persons, events, and
institutions as typologically fulfilled in Christ when the reading respects the
original context and canonical development.

Reviewed-source needs:

- primary texts: Genesis, Exodus, Psalms, Isaiah, Matthew, Luke, John, Romans,
  1 Corinthians, Hebrews, Revelation,
- Jewish and historical-critical counter-readings,
- patristic and modern theological interpretation,
- biblical-language notes on covenant, sacrifice, temple, image, word, spirit,
  and new creation.

Practical use: typology should form hope, humility, worship, and moral response,
not speculative code-breaking.

## Pack 2: Redemptive Metanarrative And Practical Theology

Claim boundary: creation, fall, covenant, redemption, restoration, and new
creation can serve as a Christian story-form for interpreting human life, but
the pattern must not force unresolved grief into premature victory.

Reviewed-source needs:

- biblical theology and canonical theology,
- theologians across eras with disagreements,
- lament and theodicy sources,
- pastoral case studies where repair is incomplete,
- communal practices of confession, reconciliation, care, and hope.

Practical use: help people locate their daily experience inside attention,
truth-telling, lament, repentance, service, and patient hope.

## Pack 3: Holy Spirit Gifts Across Peoples And Countries

Claim boundary: gifts of the Holy Spirit may be studied as signs of divine
presence and service when tested by love, truth, humility, accountability, and
fruit.

Reviewed-source needs:

- Acts, Romans 12, 1 Corinthians 12-14, Ephesians 4, Galatians 5, 1 Peter 4,
- charismatic, Pentecostal, Orthodox, Catholic, Protestant, and cautious
  cessationist readings,
- cross-cultural testimony with safeguards against manipulation,
- other-religious comparison without flattening difference,
- abuse, power, and false-discernment pressure tests.

Practical use: turn gifts toward service, wisdom, courage, healing, mercy,
truth, and community upbuilding rather than status or spectacle.

## Pack 4: Other Religious Traditions And Comparative Witness

Claim boundary: Christians may compare shared human patterns and possible
prevenient or hidden grace across religious traditions, but other traditions
must be studied on their own terms.

Reviewed-source needs:

- primary texts and expert notes from multiple traditions,
- conversion and dream testimony where available, but not as the whole lane,
- counter-readings from within each tradition,
- history of missions, colonial misuse, interreligious respect, and conflict,
- practical-theology notes on humility, witness, service, and neighbor-love.

Practical use: cultivate respectful discernment, not triumphalism.

## Pack 5: Math, Statistics, Logic, And Science Guardrails

Claim boundary: math, logic, statistics, and physics help discipline pattern
claims. They may support humility, intelligibility, and method, but they do not
prove God by themselves.

Reviewed-source needs:

- logic and inference rules,
- statistics, base rates, comparison groups, and selection-bias checks,
- qualified physics and philosophy-of-science sources,
- counterarguments to quantum overclaiming,
- clear labels for analogy, compatibility, and theological interpretation.

Practical use: slow down claims, ask better questions, and protect the project
from confusing beauty or recurrence with proof.

## Pack 6: Art, History, Language, And Human Experience

Claim boundary: visual form, historical memory, language, literature, culture,
and psychology can show how divine-pattern themes become embodied in human
experience.

Reviewed-source needs:

- art notes with actual composition, gesture, color, symbol, and context,
- history notes with conflict, power, memory, reform, and consequence,
- language notes with translation range and cultural metaphor,
- psychology notes on pattern perception, trauma, habit, desire, and repair,
- modern literature summaries and human stories with counter-readings.

Practical use: help the pattern become visible in daily love, work, grief,
justice, creativity, worship, and community formation.
