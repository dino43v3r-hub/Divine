# Source Pack: Image Of God Pattern

Source status: reviewed source pack

Claim boundary: the Image Of God pattern may connect dignity, vocation,
embodiment, symbolic capacity, moral agency, relationship, and worship. It must
not reduce personhood to intelligence, productivity, tribe, citizenship,
beauty, health, or usefulness.

Evidence:

- Genesis 1-2, Genesis 9, Psalm 8, Colossians 3, Ephesians 4, and renewal texts
  support dignity, vocation, breath, body, and restored humanity.
- Biblical-language notes on `tselem`, `demut`, breath, glory, rule, heart, and
  renewal keep word claims context-bound.
- Theologian notes from Irenaeus, Augustine, Aquinas, Zizioulas, Jennings,
  Copeland, Oduyoye, and disability/libration voices widen the doctrine.

Interpretation: Christian theology reads human dignity as gift before
performance and vocation before domination.

Discernment: every use must be tested against disability, dementia, racism,
caste, slavery, poverty, exploitation, and institutional dehumanization.

Analogy: psychology, rights discourse, kinship systems, and social cognition may
illuminate personhood but cannot replace theological anthropology.

Practical use: protect vulnerable persons, listen before judging, include weak
and overlooked people in worship, and resist usefulness-based ranking.

Failure condition: weaken or reject any version that ranks people by ability,
beauty, race, gender, class, citizenship, cognition, productivity, or religious
utility.
