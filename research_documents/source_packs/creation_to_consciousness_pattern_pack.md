# Source Pack: Creation-To-Consciousness Pattern

Source status: reviewed source pack

Claim boundary: Creation-To-Consciousness may explore ordered creation, life,
mind, moral awareness, responsibility, and worship. It must not become a
scientific proof of God or a hierarchy over bodies, animals, disabled persons,
or nonverbal life.

Evidence:

- Genesis, creation psalms, Proverbs 8, John 1, Colossians 1, Romans 8, and
  new-creation texts support creation, Logos, groaning, and hope.
- Science and deep-source guardrails support intelligibility, emergence,
  ecology, probability, and limits without turning them into proof.
- Comparative creation stories and ecological traditions widen the question
  while preserving doctrinal difference.

Interpretation: Christian theology may read the movement from creation to life,
mind, responsibility, and worship as a layered vocation under God.

Discernment: the pattern must face evolution, animal consciousness, disability,
ecological loss, waste, predation, and suffering in nature.

Analogy: emergence, cognitive science, ecology, and philosophy of mind may
explain much of the pattern without requiring theological conclusions.

Practical use: practice wonder, stewardship, embodied care, learning, ecological
repentance, and worship that honors creation without confusing it with God.

Failure condition: weaken any version that turns science into proof, ranks
consciousness as superiority, ignores animal life, or evades suffering in
creation.
