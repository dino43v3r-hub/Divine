# Source Pack: Holy Spirit Gifts Pattern

Source status: reviewed source pack

Claim boundary: gifts of the Holy Spirit may be studied as practices of service,
truth, wisdom, courage, healing, mercy, and communal upbuilding. They must not
be used for status, spectacle, control, or immunity from correction.

Evidence: Acts, Romans 12, 1 Corinthians 12-14, Ephesians 4, Galatians 5,
1 Peter 4, global testimony, Pentecostal/charismatic sources, cautious
cessationist readings, and abuse-pressure cases.

Interpretation: Christian theology may read gifts as Spirit-enabled service
within the body of Christ.

Discernment: every gift claim must be tested by love, truth, humility,
accountability, holiness, justice, and fruit over time.

Analogy: psychology of charisma, group emotion, healing expectation, social
cohesion, and other-religious experience may explain some phenomena without
settling pneumatology.

Practical use: direct gifts toward service, courage, care, truth, wisdom, and
community repair.

Failure condition: reject any gift claim that produces control, deception,
fear, exploitation, celebrity immunity, medical neglect, or contempt for the
weak.
