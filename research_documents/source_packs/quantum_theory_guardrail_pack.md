# Source Pack: Quantum Theory Guardrail

Source status: reviewed source pack

Claim boundary: quantum theory may discipline divine-pattern claims by teaching
precision about probability, measurement, uncertainty, interpretation, and model
limits. It must not be used as proof of God, prayer, consciousness, providence,
or miracles.

Evidence: quantum state formalism, Born rule probability, uncertainty
relations, measurement problem, decoherence, entanglement, Bell inequalities,
quantum field theory, quantum biology limits, and competing interpretations.

Interpretation: Christian theology may reflect on creaturely knowledge,
contingency, and humility, but only after preserving the technical source's
scope.

Discernment: every quantum claim must identify whether it is formal physics,
interpretation, metaphor, philosophy, or popularized analogy.

Analogy: uncertainty, nonclassical correlation, and probability may illuminate
humility and contingency without proving theological claims.

Practical use: slow down science overclaims, ask better questions, and protect
honest inquiry.

Failure condition: reject any use that treats "observer" as human
mind-creates-reality, entanglement as mystical connection, quantum randomness as
providence, or vacuum fluctuation as creation ex nihilo.
