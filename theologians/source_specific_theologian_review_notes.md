# Source-Specific Theologian Review Notes

Type: theologian source review
Source status: source-specific expansion, needs primary-text checking

These notes add more concrete theologian material across eras. They are not a
substitute for close reading; each claim should be checked against the named
work before it becomes strong evidence.

## Irenaeus

Primary works to review:

- *Against Heresies*
- *Demonstration of the Apostolic Preaching*

Pattern relevance:

- Creation is good and ordered, not a prison from which salvation escapes.
- Recapitulation in Christ connects creation, fall, incarnation, obedience,
  repair, and restored humanity.
- The pattern is strengthened if redemption heals creation rather than negating
  it.

Pressure point:

- Do not make Irenaeus a generic progress narrative. His argument is polemical,
  scriptural, ecclesial, and anti-gnostic.

## Athanasius

Primary works to review:

- *On the Incarnation*
- *Orations Against the Arians*

Pattern relevance:

- The Logos is not merely meaning, reason, or order. The Logos is the Son,
  active in creation and redemption.
- Incarnation links divine self-giving to human repair.
- The pattern must stay Christological rather than becoming abstract.

Pressure point:

- If the pattern can be stated without the Son's full divinity and incarnation,
  Athanasius would expose it as too thin.

## Basil of Caesarea

Primary works to review:

- *On the Holy Spirit*
- *Hexaemeron*

Pattern relevance:

- Spirit language belongs to worship, sanctification, divine agency, and the
  church's doxology.
- Creation language needs attention to order, goodness, humility, and creaturely
  limits.

Pressure point:

- Do not reduce the Holy Spirit to emotional energy, communal mood, or private
  inspiration.

## Augustine

Primary works to review:

- *Confessions*
- *De Trinitate*
- *City of God*

Pattern relevance:

- Desire, memory, restlessness, love, and grace show how human longing can be
  read theologically.
- Trinitarian analogies are useful but limited.
- History contains two loves, distorted order, suffering, and hope.

Pressure point:

- Augustine can help the pattern think about desire, but analogy cannot replace
  doctrine or lived justice.

## Aquinas

Primary works to review:

- *Summa Theologiae*
- *Summa Contra Gentiles*

Pattern relevance:

- Creation, providence, participation, virtue, grace, law, and beatitude offer a
  disciplined vocabulary for order and transformation.
- Natural reason can say some things, but revelation and grace remain distinct.

Pressure point:

- Do not turn metaphysical order into automatic proof of every theological
  claim.

## Luther

Primary works to review:

- Heidelberg Disputation
- *The Freedom of a Christian*

Pattern relevance:

- Cross, hiddenness, faith, and vocation pressure triumphal patterns.
- Transformation is received by grace and lived in service to neighbor.

Pressure point:

- A pattern that prefers visible success over the cross is being corrected by
  Luther's theology of the cross.

## Calvin

Primary works to review:

- *Institutes of the Christian Religion*

Pattern relevance:

- Providence, scripture, union with Christ, Spirit, church, and discipline offer
  a full theological architecture.
- Human knowledge of God and self must be handled with humility.

Pressure point:

- Providence must not become an excuse for institutional control or harm.

## Barth

Primary works to review:

- *Church Dogmatics*

Pattern relevance:

- Revelation is God's self-disclosure in Christ, not a pattern extracted from
  human religious experience.
- Theology must resist turning God into a general principle.

Pressure point:

- Barth challenges any version of the project that treats cross-cultural
  recurrence as sufficient for Christian doctrine.

## Bonhoeffer

Primary works to review:

- *Discipleship*
- *Life Together*
- Letters and prison writings

Pattern relevance:

- Grace, community, costly discipleship, truth, and public responsibility test
  whether transformation becomes embodied.

Pressure point:

- Cheap grace is a failure condition: transformation language without costly
  obedience and neighbor-love is too weak.

## Moltmann

Primary works to review:

- *The Crucified God*
- *Theology of Hope*

Pattern relevance:

- Suffering, cross, resurrection hope, and eschatology help the pattern resist
  shallow optimism.

Pressure point:

- Hope must face the crucified and abandoned, not erase them.

## James Cone

Primary works to review:

- *The Cross and the Lynching Tree*
- *A Black Theology of Liberation*

Pattern relevance:

- The cross must be tested against racial terror, public violence, liberation,
  and truth-telling.

Pressure point:

- Any pattern that protects power while spiritualizing suffering fails this
  witness.

## Sarah Coakley

Primary works to review:

- *God, Sexuality, and the Self*

Pattern relevance:

- Prayer, desire, vulnerability, and Trinitarian theology place transformation
  under contemplative and bodily pressure.

Pressure point:

- Desire cannot be ignored or controlled by simplistic theology.

## Willie James Jennings

Primary works to review:

- *The Christian Imagination*

Pattern relevance:

- Race, land, belonging, colonial history, and distorted Christian identity are
  central tests for any claim about communion.

Pressure point:

- Communion language fails if it ignores racialized displacement and colonial
  formation.
