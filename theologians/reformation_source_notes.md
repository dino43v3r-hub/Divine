# Reformation Source Notes

Source lane: theologians
Era: Reformation
Source status: source reviewed

## Martin Luther

Primary source focus: *The Freedom of a Christian*, Heidelberg Disputation,
Galatians lectures.

Pattern contribution: the theology of the cross challenges any pattern that
only recognizes God in success, visible order, or triumph. Grace, faith, lament,
hiddenness, and suffering must be allowed to unsettle shallow synthesis.

Risk: do not make suffering good in itself. The cross exposes false glory and
points to God's mercy, not romanticized pain.

## John Calvin

Primary source focus: *Institutes of the Christian Religion*.

Pattern contribution: creation, providence, scripture, union with Christ, church,
discipline, and public life belong together. Calvin helps the app test whether a
pattern becomes practical in worship, moral formation, and communal order.

Risk: providence must not become fatalism or an excuse for injustice.

## Teresa Of Avila And John Of The Cross

Primary source focus: interior prayer, purgation, union, and dark night.

Pattern contribution: spiritual transformation includes desire, discipline,
darkness, purification, love, and embodied practice over time.

Risk: mystical language needs pastoral care. Darkness can be spiritual, but it
can also involve illness, trauma, abuse, or depression that requires practical
help.
